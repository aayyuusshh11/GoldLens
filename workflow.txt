### 2026-10-20 (7th pass) � answered the 7 quant questions in teammate docs/HANDOFF.md (aayyuusshh11/GoldLens, commit 8af768c)
The frontend team lead's handoff flags open contract questions for this module. Verified answers
directly from the local implementation (cite these back; they mirror INTEGRATION_BLOCKERS.md):
1. Spread sign convention: spread = far_close - near_close (quant/spreads.pair_spreads).
   Positive far premium = contango. SIGN CONFIRMED.
2. win_rate IS A FRACTION in [0,1] (wins/n_trades); sharpe_ratio is ANNUALIZED daily
   P&L: mean/std * sqrt(252), using TRADING-day series (quant/metrics.compute_metrics;
   gross and net_* variants; withheld below min_days_for_sharpe=20 or zero std). CONFIRMED.
3. walk_forward_cmp inner keys (quant.engine.compare_walk_forward): fold, sessions, trades,
   basis ("net"/"gross_only_costs_missing"), gross_pnl, net_pnl, win_rate, max_drawdown,
   sharpe, delta_gross_vs_prev_fold, dates:(start,end). Chronological; held-out tail per fold.
4. quote_basis_g: contracts are quoted in Rs per 10 GRAM; quote_basis_g=10 for GOLD and
   GOLDM. rupee value of 1 lot = close * (lot_size_g / quote_basis_g) = close * rvalue
   (GOLD 100, GOLDM 10). That is the spec-check identity, cross-checked vs the YearWise file.
5. stale flag: row has blank/zero pricing fields but was kept (loader marks and KEEPS such
   rows; selection may disqualify them via invalid_price/no_valid_price_or_expiry reasons;
   SpreadPointDTO.stale reproduces the loader flag). Pair/expiry selection: select_pair runs
   per trading date over that day's eligible contracts (nearest two eligible expiries, near
